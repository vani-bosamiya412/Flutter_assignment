1.  App Release Process for iOS and Android

iOS (Apple): - You need an Apple Developer Account. - Create an App ID
and set up certificates and provisioning profiles. - Build the app in
release mode using Xcode or Flutter. - Upload the build to App Store
Connect using Xcode or Transporter. - Add app information: screenshots,
description, category, privacy policy. - Submit the app for review.
After approval, it will go live on the App Store.

Android (Google Play): - You need a Google Play Developer Account. -
Create a new app in Google Play Console. - Generate an Android App
Bundle (AAB) in release mode. - Upload the AAB to Google Play Console. -
Add required assets: screenshots, description, icons, feature
graphics. - Submit for review. After approval, it will be published.

2.  Steps to Generate App Bundles and APKs

APK (Android Package): - APK is used mostly for testing. - Run: flutter
build apk –release - The output APK will be in
build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/

App Bundle (AAB) – Recommended for Play Store: - AAB is required for
uploading to Google Play. - Run: flutter build appbundle –release - The
output AAB will be in build/app/outputs/bundle/release/

iOS Build: - Run: flutter build ios –release - Open
ios/Runner.xcworkspace in Xcode. - Archive the project and upload to App
Store Connect.

3.  Best Practices for Submitting Apps

App Store (iOS): - Use clear and high-quality screenshots. - Make sure
your app follows Apple’s design and privacy rules. - Test the app
thoroughly on real devices. - Write a clear description and app
purpose. - Add a strong privacy policy.

Google Play (Android): - Optimize app performance to avoid crashes. -
Use good-quality graphics and screenshots. - Follow Play Store content
policies. - Add a proper app icon and feature graphic. - Test on
different Android devices before uploading.
