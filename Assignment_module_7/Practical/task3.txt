Steps to Publish a Flutter App on the Google Play Store

1.  Prepare Your App for Release

-   Update your app version in pubspec.yaml.
-   Remove debug logs and test code.
-   Add app icons using the flutter_launcher_icons package.
-   Ensure the app works properly on real devices.

2.  Create a Google Play Developer Account

-   Go to Google Play Console.
-   Pay the one-time registration fee.
-   Complete the account setup.

3.  Create a New Application

-   Click “Create App” in Play Console.
-   Select your app name, default language, and app type.
-   Accept all policies.

4.  App Bundle Generation (AAB)

-   Open your Flutter project.
-   Run: flutter build appbundle –release
-   The AAB file will be in: build/app/outputs/bundle/release/

5.  App Signing Setup

-   Google Play uses Play App Signing by default.
-   If needed, generate your keystore using: keytool -genkey -v
    -keystore key.jks -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias
    upload
-   Add the keystore path and passwords inside android/key.properties.
-   Flutter will use this automatically during the build.

6.  Upload the AAB to Google Play Console

-   Go to “Production” → “Create New Release”.
-   Upload your .aab file.
-   Add Release Notes.

7.  Complete App Details

-   App name, short description, long description
-   App category: App or Game + Free or Paid
-   Contact details (email required)
-   Privacy policy URL (must be valid)

8.  Add Store Listing Assets

-   App icon (512×512)
-   Feature graphic (1024×500)
-   Screenshots for phone and tablet
-   Optional: Promo video

9.  Fill Content Rating Questionnaire

-   Answer questions honestly.
-   Generate rating.

10. Set Up App Pricing and Distribution

-   Choose countries where the app should be available.
-   Select whether it’s free or paid.

11. Submit for Review

-   Go to “Production” → “Review and rollout”.
-   Submit the app for Google review.
-   Approval usually takes a few hours to a few days.

12. After Approval

-   Your app becomes publicly available on the Google Play Store.
